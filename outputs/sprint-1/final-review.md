**Sprint:** Sprint 1 - Product Foundation

## Objective

Sprint 1 moved WPSC from framework-only development toward product readiness.

The goal was:

> A developer who has never used WPSC should be able to install the framework, connect it to WordPress, and create the first website without reading source code.
